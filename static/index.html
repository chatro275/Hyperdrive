Microphone settings

Two independent microphones can hear commands. The first wake detection owns the command; audio is not mixed. Two recognizers use more Pi CPU.

Try: “Hey Hyper, storage space”, “create folder called holidays”, “list files”, “skip forward thirty seconds”, “subtitles on”. Feedback appears on screen.

Speak near the mic to check the meter. Refresh after plugging in a new mic; the voice service will briefly restart. Device numbers may change after reboot.

Access away from home

Upload and download using a private connection to your Pi. Install Tailscale on your Pi and each device you use, and sign in to the same personal account.

Check whether your Pi is connected.

First-time setup
  1. On the Pi, open a terminal in your HyperMedia folder and run:
    bash scripts/install-pi.sh --remote-access
    Follow the sign-in link shown in the terminal.
  2. Install Tailscale on your Mac or phone and sign in to the same account.
  3. Connect Tailscale, then select Check connection here and bookmark the address.

The Pi and USB drive must remain online. Access is limited by your Tailscale network permissions. Only grant trusted devices access: they can manage this dashboard. Do not forward port 8080 on your router or expose this portal using a public tunnel.
